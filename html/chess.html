Chess Rules

Objective: Checkmate your opponent's king.

Pieces & Movements:
  • King (♔/♚): Moves one square in any direction. Cannot move into check.
  • Queen (♕/♛): Moves any number of squares in any direction.
  • Rook (♖/♜): Moves any number of squares horizontally or vertically.
  • Bishop (♗/♝): Moves any number of squares diagonally.
  • Knight (♘/♞): Moves in an L-shape: two squares in one direction, then one square perpendicular. Can jump over pieces.
  • Pawn (♙/♟): Moves forward one square (or two from its starting position). Captures diagonally. Can promote to another piece upon reaching the last rank.
Special Moves:
  • Castling: King moves two squares towards a rook, and the rook moves to the square next to the king. Conditions: neither piece has moved, squares between are empty, king is not in check or passing through check.
  • En Passant: Pawn can capture an opponent's pawn that has just moved two squares forward from its starting position, as if it had moved only one square.
  • Pawn Promotion: When a pawn reaches the last rank, it is promoted to a queen, rook, bishop, or knight.
Game Rules:
  • Check: The king is under attack. You must move out of check.
  • Checkmate: The king is in check and cannot escape. The game ends.
  • Stalemate: The player has no legal moves and is not in check. The game is a draw.
  • Draws: Can occur by stalemate, insufficient material, threefold repetition, or the fifty-move rule.
How to Win:
  • Deliver checkmate to your opponent's king.
  • Force a resignation.
Tips:
  • Control the center of the board.
  • Develop your pieces early.
  • Protect your king (castle early).
  • Watch for tactics: forks, pins, skewers.
